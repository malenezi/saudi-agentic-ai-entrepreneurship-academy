# ورقة شروط التجربة المدفوعة · Paid pilot terms sheet

<div dir="rtl">

> **تنبيه قانوني:** هذه ورقة تسجّل ما اتفق عليه الطرفان في النقاش، ولم تُعدّ لتكون عقداً ولا نموذج عقد، ولم يراجعها محامٍ. وهل يترتب على توقيعها أثر نظامي؟ سؤال للمحامي قبل أن يوقّع أحد. لا تقترح حكماً في أي بند: الخانات تُملأ بما اتفقتما عليه، والصياغة الملزمة يكتبها محامٍ مرخّص في المملكة. لا توقّعا مستنداً ملزماً مبنياً عليها قبل مراجعته.

</div>

> **Legal note.** This sheet records what two parties agreed in discussion. It is not meant as a contract or a contract template, and no lawyer has reviewed it. Whether signing it has legal effect is a question for the lawyer before anyone signs. It proposes no position on any item: the blanks hold what you agreed, and a licensed Saudi lawyer writes the binding wording. Do not sign a binding document built on it before that review.

**How it differs from [template 9](09-pilot-and-loi-outline.md).** Template 9 is your side: the pilot you design and the offer you send. This sheet is filled **with the customer**, after they say yes in principle, and it adds what an agent needs that an ordinary software trial does not: stages of autonomy, who approves what, who can stop it, and what happens to the data. It is the brief you hand the lawyer. Explained in [Module 3.6](../03-courses/03-launch/lessons/3-6-pilots-and-letters-of-intent.md).
