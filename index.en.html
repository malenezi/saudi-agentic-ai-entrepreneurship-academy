Saudi Agentic AI Entrepreneurship Academy

An open-source, Arabic-first reference for anyone who wants to start, run or grow a business in Saudi Arabia built on agentic AI: four courses, a Saudi market knowledge framework, a founder toolkit and a lab.

الأكاديمية السعودية لريادة الأعمال بالذكاء الاصطناعي الوكيلي

Read the overview Repository on GitHub العربية

An independent personal initiative, founded and maintained by Prof. Mamdouh Alenezi. It is not a government body and represents none. The content is educational, not legal, tax or financial advice.

What you will find

Everything is in the repository, and every page opens on this site. Arabic is the Academy's first language: lessons and slide decks are in Arabic with English terms; syllabi, the trainer guide, the knowledge framework's domain pages and the lab code are in English or in both.

  • Course 1: Finding your agentic opportunitySeven lessons that end in an opportunity thesis backed by customer interviews.
  • Course 2: Building AI agentsEight lessons that end in a working agent and the evidence that it works.
  • Course 3: Launch and business modelSeven lessons that end in a first product used by a real customer and a price computed from the log.
  • Course 4: Scale and governSix lessons that end in an operating architecture, a risk register and an investor pitch.
  • Programme trainer guideWhat a trainer or an organisation needs to deliver the four courses: requirements, assessment, preparation.
  • Saudi market knowledge frameworkTwelve domains, among them establishment, data, tax and funding, with a source for every fact.
  • Founder toolkitFourteen bilingual business templates and four calculators.
  • Agentic AI labSkills, agent templates, an evaluation template and seventeen exercises.
  • Venture workbookArabicThe gates of the four courses on one page, with evidence for each item. Saved in your browser and exported as a text file.
  • ContributeEvery contribution is welcome, and you do not need to be a programmer.

Where it stands

All four courses have their first content. No course has been piloted with a cohort, no founder has used the founder agents, and no lawyer, tax adviser or security specialist has reviewed the regulatory content.

  • RoadmapWhat each release added, the known gaps, and where help matters most.
  • Release readinessThe status of every gap, course by course.
  • Source registerEvery external fact, its source, and the date it was checked.
