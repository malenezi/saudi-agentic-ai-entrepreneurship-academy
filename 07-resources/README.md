## 6. International programmes we learned from

The academy's design borrows ideas from university entrepreneurship programmes, founder schools, venture builders and agentic AI courses in several countries: the workflow wedge, shipping before specifying, unit economics for AI products, mixed technical and domain teams, and Demo Days judged on customer evidence. Programme details change every year, so we do not reproduce them here; they are tracked as items to verify in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md), section C.
