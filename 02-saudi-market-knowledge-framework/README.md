# Saudi Market Knowledge Framework

**Saudi Entrepreneur's Knowledge and Compliance Hub**

> The courses teach founders **what** to do. This framework tells them **how** to do it in Saudi Arabia. The agents help them **execute**.

A first-class product of the academy, not supplementary reading. It gives any entrepreneur a practical reference for establishing, operating, financing, protecting and scaling a business in the Kingdom, with particular attention to agentic AI ventures.

**Status:** v0.2 · Last reviewed 16 November 2026 · **Every contribution is welcome**, large or small ([how to contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## The content standard

Every domain is built toward the same eight resource types:

| # | Resource | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Regulatory library | Applicable laws, regulations and official decisions, each entered in the register |
| 2 | Founder guides | Step-by-step explanations in plain Arabic and English |
| 3 | Practical checklists | Actions a founder can take this week |
| 4 | Templates and documents | Reusable policies, agreements and models |
| 5 | Tools and calculators | Readiness, cost and risk tools |
| 6 | Case studies | Real Saudi scenarios (illustrative ones are labelled) |
| 7 | Official references | Pointers to the issuing authority |
| 8 | AI skills and agents | Prompts and agent definitions that apply the domain |

## Four labels we never mix

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Binding** | Law, regulation or mandatory control for those in scope | Personal Data Protection Law |
| **Official guidance** | Published by an authority, not binding in itself | AI Ethics Principles |
| **Draft** | Consultation text, not adopted | Draft Global AI Hub Law |
| **Academy practice** | Our recommendation or template | Human oversight matrix |

A binding instrument still applies only to the entities and activities in its scope. Teach founders to ask "does this apply to **me**?" before "what does it require?": by entity type, by customer, by sector and by contract.

## Maintenance rules

1. Nothing is presented as current unless its official source has been checked; the check date is recorded in the [register](regulatory-register.md).
2. Drafts are labelled as drafts.
3. Funding programmes are never shown as open without checking; eligibility, amounts and windows are quoted only from the operator.
4. No one is told they are "compliant". The framework helps founders prepare; determinations belong to authorities and licensed advisers.
5. Any reader can correct anything through a `resource-update` issue.
