## Keeping this page honest

- Addresses were written from general knowledge on the review date and have not each been opened in this iteration. If one is wrong or has moved, please open a `resource-update` issue.
- We link to home pages on purpose. Deep links break; search the authority's site for the named document.
- Suggest a missing authority with one line on why a founder needs it.
