## Keeping this page honest

- Every address was opened on 20 November 2026, and four were corrected: NTDP, CMA, Kafalah and the Saudi Business Center. Opening a site confirms the address and who runs it, not every service named in the last column. If an address is wrong or has moved, please open a `resource-update` issue.
- We link to home pages on purpose. Deep links break; search the authority's site for the named document.
- Suggest a missing authority with one line on why a founder needs it.
