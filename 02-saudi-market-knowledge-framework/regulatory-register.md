## How to read it

**Legal status:** Binding · Official guidance · Draft
**Verification:**
- ✔ **Confirmed**: checked on the review date against the issuer or at least one reputable legal or news source (reference in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md)).
- **No "to verify" rows.** Since 20 November 2026 the register keeps only what was confirmed. A row confirms that the instrument exists and what its status is; where the note says "not summarised here" or "texts not read", the register says nothing about its provisions.
- ✖ **Not found**: appeared in an early draft and could not be confirmed. Do not rely on it.

"Binding" always means *binding on those in scope*. Check applicability to your entity, customers, sector and contracts.
