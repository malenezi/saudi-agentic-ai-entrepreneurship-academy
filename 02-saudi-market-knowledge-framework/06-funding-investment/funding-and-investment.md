## 1. Choose a path first

| Path | Fits when | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue-funded** | You sell an outcome to SMEs, costs are mostly variable, and customers pay from the first month | Slower growth; full control |
| **Grants and support programmes** | You are pre-revenue with a prototype and need time, tools or credits | Application effort; conditions attached |
| **Incubator or accelerator** | You need structure, mentors and introductions more than cash | Time commitment; sometimes equity |
| **Angel or venture investment** | The market is large, speed matters and there is a real moat to build | Dilution; growth expectations |
| **Debt and guarantees** | You have revenue and need working capital | Repayment obligation |

Market context: Saudi startups raised $1.72 billion in 2025 across 257 deals, up 145% year on year, according to MAGNiTT ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), A2).

## 3. What investors ask an agentic startup

Prepare evidence-backed answers to these before any meeting.

| Question | Where your answer comes from |
|---|---|
| Who is the customer and what proof do you have? | Course 1 thesis; Course 3 pilot data |
| Does it work? | Success rate on your evaluation set and in production |
| What does one completed task cost and earn? | [Unit economics](../../04-entrepreneur-toolkit/06-unit-economics.md) |
| What happens to your margin if model prices change or you switch models? | Sensitivity table from the calculator |
| What do you own that the next model release does not erase? | Workflow depth, proprietary data, integrations, distribution, trust |
| How do you handle personal data and where is it processed? | [PDPL self-assessment](../compliance-toolkit/pdpl-self-assessment.md) |
| What stops the agent doing something harmful? | [Human oversight matrix](../compliance-toolkit/human-oversight-matrix.md), security assessment |
| Why this team? | Domain access plus ability to build |

## بالعربية: خمس نصائح قبل طلب التمويل

1. اختر المسار أولاً: الإيرادات، الدعم الحكومي، الحاضنة، الاستثمار، أو التمويل بالدَّين.
2. لا تطلب مالاً لتعرف هل يريد العميل منتجك؛ اعرف ذلك أولاً بتجربة مدفوعة.
3. احسب تكلفة المهمة المنجزة بدقة، فهو أول ما يسألك عنه مستثمر يفهم المجال.
4. جهّز جوابك عن سؤال الميزة التنافسية: ما الذي تملكه ولا يمحوه إصدار نموذج جديد؟
5. اجعل الامتثال جزءاً من عرضك، فهو عنصر ثقة عند المشتري والمستثمر.
