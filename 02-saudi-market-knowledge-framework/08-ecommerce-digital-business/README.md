## Authorities

Ministry of Commerce (e-commerce and consumer protection), ZATCA (VAT and invoicing), SAMA (payments), CST (certain digital platforms), SDAIA (personal data and marketing consent).

## Instruments

| Instrument | Authority | Status | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| E-Commerce Law and Implementing Regulations | Ministry of Commerce | Binding | ◻ |
| Consumer protection and anti-commercial-fraud rules | Ministry of Commerce | Binding | ◻ |
| Electronic transactions framework | — | Binding | ◻ |
| Payment services rules; licensed payment providers | SAMA | Binding | ◻ |
| Advertising licence for individuals advertising on social media | Media regulator | Binding | ◻ |
| Regulations for Providing Digital Content Platform Services | CST | Binding for content platforms | ✔ in force 8 October 2024 |
| PDPL conditions on direct marketing | SDAIA | Binding | ◻ |

## What changes when an agent sells or serves

| Agent activity | Check |
|---|---|
| Answers product questions | Statements about price, availability and features bind the store. Answer from the catalogue, never from the model's memory |
| Recommends products | Recommendations must not mislead; disclose sponsored placement |
| Generates advertising | Claims must be true; follow advertising rules and cultural standards |
| Sends offers by message | Consent and opt-out for marketing under PDPL; messaging-platform policies |
| Handles returns and complaints | Follow the published policy and the consumer's statutory rights; escalate exceptions |
| Takes payment | Use a licensed payment provider; the agent should not hold card data |

## Founder decisions

| Decision | Ask |
|---|---|
| What I am selling | Product, digital good, software subscription or a service performed by agents? |
| Store disclosures | Are business identity, contact details, prices, terms and return policy published? |
| Payments | Which licensed provider, and what recurring-billing rules apply? |
| Cross-border | Am I selling to customers outside the Kingdom, or buying from suppliers abroad? |

## Starter checklist

- [ ] Commercial registration covers the online activity
- [ ] Store shows business identity, contact, prices including VAT, terms and return policy
- [ ] Privacy notice in Arabic
- [ ] Licensed payment provider integrated
- [ ] Compliant invoices issued
- [ ] Marketing consent recorded; easy opt-out
- [ ] Agent discloses that it is automated and can hand over to a person
- [ ] Agent answers on price and policy only from approved content
