## What the E-Commerce Law says

Read in the Arabic text on the Bureau of Experts' site on 22 November 2026 (A157). A summary for orientation: the text and its implementing regulation decide, and the regulation was not read.

| Topic | What the law says | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Who it covers | A service provider inside the Kingdom, a practitioner outside it who offers products or services inside the Kingdom, and the consumer | 2 |
| Consumer data | Keep it only as long as the transaction requires, protect it, and do not use it for other purposes or disclose it without the consumer's consent | 5 |
| What the store shows | Name, address, means of contact, and the register and number where it is registered | 6 |
| Before the contract | A statement of the terms: the steps to conclude it, the provider's details, the essential features of the product or service, the total price with all fees, taxes and delivery charges, the arrangements for payment, delivery and performance, and the warranty if any | 7 |
| Advertising | An electronic advertisement is a contractual document that binds the parties. It names the product or service and the provider and gives means of contact. False or misleading claims, and marks the provider has no right to use, are prohibited | 10, 11 |
| Termination by the consumer | Within seven days of receiving the product, or of contracting for a service, if it has not been used or benefited from. The consumer bears the cost of termination unless otherwise agreed | 13(1) |
| No termination right | Products made to the consumer's order; videos, discs, CDs and software that have been used; newspapers, magazines, publications and books; defects caused by the consumer's misuse; accommodation, transport and catering services; software downloaded online; other cases set by the regulation | 13(2) |
| Late delivery | Unless another period is agreed, a delay of more than 15 days lets the consumer terminate and recover what was paid and the costs the delay caused. The provider must tell the consumer of an expected delay | 14 |
| Penalties | A warning, a fine of up to SAR 1,000,000, suspension of the activity, blocking of the store | 18 |

For an agent this fixes three things. What it advertises binds the store (Article 10). The seven days, the exceptions and the 15 days are the floor under whatever the published policy says, so the agent sends the policy and hands a dispute to a person. And the total price it quotes includes fees, taxes and delivery (Article 7).

## What changes when an agent sells or serves

| Agent activity | Check |
|---|---|
| Answers product questions | Statements about price, availability and features bind the store. Answer from the catalogue, never from the model's memory |
| Recommends products | Recommendations must not mislead; disclose sponsored placement |
| Generates advertising | Claims must be true; follow advertising rules and cultural standards |
| Sends offers by message | Consent and opt-out for marketing under PDPL; messaging-platform policies |
| Handles returns and complaints | Follow the published policy and the consumer's statutory rights; escalate exceptions |
| Takes payment | Use a licensed payment provider; the agent should not hold card data |

## Founder decisions

| Decision | Ask |
|---|---|
| What I am selling | Product, digital good, software subscription or a service performed by agents? |
| Store disclosures | Are business identity, contact details, prices, terms and return policy published? |
| Payments | Which licensed provider, and what recurring-billing rules apply? |
| Cross-border | Am I selling to customers outside the Kingdom, or buying from suppliers abroad? |

## Starter checklist

- [ ] Commercial registration covers the online activity
- [ ] Store shows business identity, contact, prices including VAT, terms and return policy
- [ ] Privacy notice in Arabic
- [ ] Licensed payment provider integrated
- [ ] Compliant invoices issued
- [ ] Marketing consent recorded; easy opt-out
- [ ] Agent discloses that it is automated and can hand over to a person
- [ ] Agent answers on price and policy only from approved content
