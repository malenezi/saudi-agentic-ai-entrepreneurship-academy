## Saudi AI Startup Opportunity Map (format)

Each opportunity is published with ten fields. Empty fields are invitations.

1. Industry
2. Problem
3. Target customer
4. Existing alternatives
5. AI agent opportunity
6. Required technical capabilities
7. Regulatory implications (link to [domain 10](../10-sector-regulations/README.md))
8. Candidate business model
9. Potential competitors
10. Evidence and source links, with dates

Initial priorities: healthcare administration, tourism, logistics, professional services, retail and SME operations.

## Rules for market claims

- Quote the publisher, the report and the period. Add the entry to [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).
- Prefer bottom-up counts of reachable customers to top-down market sizes.
- Separate what customers **did** from what they **said**.
- Label estimates as estimates and show the arithmetic.
- Anonymise field evidence before sharing.
