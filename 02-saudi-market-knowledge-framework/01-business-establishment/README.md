## Founder decisions

| Decision | Why it matters | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form: sole establishment, limited liability company, simplified joint-stock company | Liability, ability to take investors, ability to issue shares to employees | Will I raise investment? Do I have co-founders? |
| Economic activity classification | Determines licences and which regulator you meet | Am I selling software, or performing a regulated service through software? |
| Founders' agreement | Most early disputes are about equity, roles and exits | Who owns what, what vests when, what happens if someone leaves? |
| IP assignment into the company | Investors check that the company owns the code and brand | Was anything built before incorporation or by contractors? See [domain 07](../07-intellectual-property/README.md) |
| Foreign founders or investors | Changes the registration path | Is any founder or shareholder non-Saudi? |

## Starter checklist

- [ ] Choose the legal form with advice if you plan to raise investment
- [ ] Reserve the trade name and check the trademark at the same time
- [ ] Select the correct economic activity
- [ ] Complete commercial registration
- [ ] Sign the founders' agreement and assign IP to the company
- [ ] Open a business bank account
- [ ] Register with ZATCA and assess VAT obligations ([domain 05](../05-tax-accounting-finance/README.md))
- [ ] Register with GOSI and the labour platforms before the first hire ([domain 09](../09-employment-hr/README.md))
- [ ] Register a national address for the business
- [ ] Note the annual obligations that follow registration
