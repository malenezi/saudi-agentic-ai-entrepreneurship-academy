## Why it matters now

The law is binding and enforced. In early 2026 SDAIA announced that its violation-review committees had issued 48 decisions confirming violations over the previous year (see [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)). It covers small businesses that process personal data.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Status | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19, 2021, as amended) | Binding | ✔ enforcement · ◻ article-level detail |
| Implementing Regulation | Binding | ◻ |
| Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom | Binding | ◻ |
| SDAIA guide to the PDPL for controllers and processors | Official guidance | ◻ |

One early draft cited the law as "Royal Decree M/19 of 2024". The decree dates from 2021; the law became enforceable in September 2024 after its transition period.

## Worked example to build (wanted)

A Saudi startup runs a customer-service agent connected to a CRM holding names, phone numbers, purchase history and conversations. The finished example should answer, in a filled-in self-assessment:

1. What data is collected, and why?
2. Who is the controller and who is the processor?
3. Which external providers receive the data?
4. Does any of it leave the Kingdom?
5. How long is each category kept?
6. How does a customer exercise their rights?
