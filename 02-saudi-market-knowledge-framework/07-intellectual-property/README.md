## Authority

Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP). Under the new Copyright Law some matters are distributed to other authorities.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Status | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Copyright Law, Royal Decree M/169 | Binding | ✔ in force 12 August 2026 |
| Copyright Implementing Regulations | Binding | ✔ published 31 July 2026 |
| GCC Trademark Law and its implementing regulation | Binding | ✔ listed among SAIP's laws and regulations (A136); text not read here |
| Law of Patents, Layout Designs of Integrated Circuits, Plant Varieties and Industrial Designs | Binding | ✔ listed among SAIP's laws and regulations (A136); text not read here |
| Regulation for the Protection of Confidential Commercial Information | Binding | ✔ listed among SAIP's laws and regulations, published 27 June 2022 (A136); text not read here |

## The AI training exception

The new law introduces a statutory exception for reproducing works in AI training and development, described as one of the first in the Gulf. Legal commentary on the Implementing Regulations stresses that it is **narrow and conditional**: it covers lawfully published works that were lawfully acquired, the copying must be necessary for AI development, and developers must meet conditions on data use, record-keeping and commercial exploitation. Some points remain unclear and may need further guidance from SAIP.

**What this means for a founder fine-tuning on Arabic content:** do not assume the exception covers your use. Record where every training dataset came from and on what terms, and take legal advice before relying on it. Sources are in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).

## What a technology founder should protect

| Asset | Protection | First step |
|---|---|---|
| Brand name and logo | Trademark | Search and file before launch |
| Code and original content | Copyright; contracts | Make sure the company, not individuals, owns it |
| Algorithms, prompts, evaluation sets, workflows | Trade secret | Access controls and confidentiality terms |
| Qualifying inventions | Patent | Seek advice before any public disclosure |
| Data you collect | Contract and data rights | Clear terms with customers |

## Founder decisions

| Decision | Ask |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Did every founder, employee and contractor assign their work to the company in writing? |
| Open source | Which licences are in my product, and what do they oblige me to do? |
| Model licences | Does each model's licence allow my commercial use? |
| AI-generated output | Do my customer terms say who owns outputs? |
| Training data | Do I have the right to use it, and can I prove it? |

## Open-source licences in one table

| Licence | You may use it commercially | Main obligation |
|---|---|---|
| MIT | Yes | Keep the copyright notice |
| Apache-2.0 | Yes | Keep notices; includes a patent grant |
| GPL family | Yes | If you distribute a derived work, release its source under the same licence |
| Creative Commons | Depends on the variant | For content, not software; "NC" variants forbid commercial use |
| Custom model licences | Read each one | May restrict commercial use, scale or field |
