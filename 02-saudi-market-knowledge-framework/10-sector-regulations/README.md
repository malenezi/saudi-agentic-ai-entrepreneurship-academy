## Rule of thumb

**Identify your primary risk regulator first**, then sequence the others. The same agent meets very different expectations depending on its use.

Two distinctions that decide the pathway:

- **Software for a regulated firm versus a regulated service.** Selling software to a bank is not necessarily providing a financial service. The actual activity and business model determine the route.
- **Administration versus clinical function.** Automating a clinic's scheduling differs from software intended for diagnosis or treatment, which may need a separate regulatory assessment as a medical device.

## Regulatory sandboxes

A sandbox lets a company test a product under supervision with adapted requirements. Choose by your primary regulator.

| Sandbox | Regulator | Focus | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emerging Technologies Regulatory Sandbox | CST | AI, Internet of Things, digital twin, space and other emerging technologies | ✔ Fifteen products completed the second cohort; registration for the third ran from 3 July to 3 October 2025 |

Early drafts quoted typical sandbox durations and participant counts. They could not be confirmed and are omitted. Check each regulator's current call for applications.

For an agentic product that fits no licensed sector, the CST sandbox is a natural place to look first, because it accepts emerging-technology products that are not yet specifically regulated.

## Founder decisions

| Decision | Ask |
|---|---|
| Am I regulated? | Do I perform the regulated activity, or supply a tool to someone who does? |
| Primary regulator | Whose rules carry my biggest risk? |
| Partner or licence | Can I work through a licensed partner first? |
| Sandbox | Does my product need supervised testing before market? |
| Evidence | What will the regulator or licensed partner ask me to show? |
