## The layered model

```
 Sector rules        finance · health · real estate · education ...   (binding where licensed)
 SDAIA guidance      ethics principles · GenAI guidelines · adoption · risk management
 Binding base        PDPL · cybersecurity controls · copyright · commerce law
```

There is no single AI statute. Binding obligations come mainly from data protection, cybersecurity, intellectual property and sector law; SDAIA's AI instruments sit on top as guidance that buyers use in procurement.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Authority | Status | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Ethics Principles 2.0 (September 2023): seven principles, risk-tiered | SDAIA | Official guidance | ✔ |
| Generative AI Guidelines (one version for government, one for the public) | SDAIA | Official guidance | ✔ both on SDAIA's site; issued early 2024 according to SDAIA's own report (A127) |
| AI Adoption Framework (September 2024) | SDAIA | Official guidance | ✔ |
| National AI Risk Management Framework (April 2026) | SDAIA | Official guidance | ✔ |
| AI Cybersecurity Guidelines (AICG-1:2026) | NCA | **Draft**, non-binding | ✔ status |
| Draft Global AI Hub Law | CST | **Draft** | ✔ published for consultation on 14 April 2025, feedback until 14 May 2025 (A135) |
| Copyright Law with AI training exception (in force 12 August 2026) | SAIP | Binding | ✔ |

## Founder decisions

| Decision | Ask |
|---|---|
| Risk level of each agent | Does it only recommend, or does it act? On money, records, or people's rights? |
| Disclosure | Where and how are users told they are dealing with an agent? |
| Accountable owner | Who is named for each agent and each change? |
| Human approval thresholds | Which actions pause for a person? |
| Evidence | What can I show a buyer today? See the [compliance checklist](../founder-compliance-checklist.md) |
