# سجل المدفوعات للمزوّدين الأجانب · Foreign supplier payment register

Educational template, not tax advice. One row per non-resident supplier you pay: model providers, cloud, software subscriptions, messaging, analytics, consultants. Read [Tax and zakat basics, section 4](tax-basics-for-founders.md) first.

**The rule of this register:** the classification column stays "awaiting adviser review" until a licensed tax adviser has decided it and signed the row. A suggestion from an AI assistant does not change the status.
