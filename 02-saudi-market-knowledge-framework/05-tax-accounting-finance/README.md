## Authority

ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) administers VAT, zakat, income tax, withholding tax, e-invoicing and customs.

## Instruments

| Subject | Instrument | Status | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| VAT | VAT Law and Implementing Regulations | Binding | ✔ thresholds (A53) · ✔ standard rate 15% (A138) |
| E-invoicing | FATOORA regulations and integration requirements | Binding | ✔ wave 25, announced 24 July 2026 (A54) |
| Zakat | Implementing Regulations for Zakat Collection (Minister of Finance Decision 1007 of 19/08/1445H, as amended) | Binding | ✔ who is subject, the 2.5% rate, registration and the 120-day return, from ZATCA's text (A152). How the zakat base is computed is not summarised |
| Income tax | Income Tax Law (Royal Decree M/1 of 15/1/1425H) | Binding | ✔ in force on the Bureau of Experts' site; applies to a resident capital company on the shares of non-Saudi owners; rate 20%; registration and the 120-day return (A153). How the tax base is computed is not summarised |
| Withholding tax | Rules on payments to non-residents | Binding | ✔ rates and deadlines from ZATCA's General Guideline, May 2026 (A55). How software, cloud and API payments are classified is the adviser's decision |

## Points every agentic founder should check with an adviser

The VAT thresholds, the e-invoicing wave and the withholding rates were checked against ZATCA's pages on 18 November 2026 ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), A53 to A55). Rows that could not be confirmed were removed on 20 November 2026. Zakat and income tax were restored on 22 November 2026 from ZATCA's text of the zakat regulations and the Bureau of Experts' text of the Income Tax Law (A152, A153). **Confirm each point with ZATCA or a licensed tax adviser before relying on it.**

| Topic | What to check | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| VAT registration | Mandatory registration above SAR 375,000 of taxable supplies in twelve months; voluntary above SAR 187,500 (A53). How the test looks backward and forward is for the adviser | ✔ thresholds |
| VAT on what you sell | Whether your service is standard-rated (the standard rate is 15%, A138), and how subscriptions and usage fees are invoiced | ✔ rate. The rest is a question for the adviser |
| Zakat or income tax | Which applies to each owner's share. Zakat at 2.5% of the zakat base applies to a company in respect of the shares of Saudi partners, and the regulations treat GCC nationals accorded the same treatment as Saudi (A152). Income tax at 20% of the tax base applies to a resident capital company in respect of shares owned by non-Saudi persons (A153). A company with both kinds of owner comes under both, each for its share. Either way: register with ZATCA before the end of the first fiscal year, and file and pay within 120 days of the year's end. How each base is computed, and what changes when an investor joins during the year, is the adviser's question | ✔ who is subject, the rates and the deadlines |
| Imported services | How VAT applies to model APIs, cloud and software bought from foreign suppliers | A question for the adviser; nothing is stated here |
| Withholding tax | Which rate applies to each foreign supplier: 5% technical and consulting services, 15% royalties, 15% any other payments, 20% management fees (A55). The classification is the adviser's decision | ✔ rates |
| E-invoicing | Whether you fall in wave 25 (VAT-subject revenue above SAR 187,500 in any of 2022 to 2025; integrate by 1 February 2027) or an earlier wave (A54) | ✔ wave 25 |

- [ ] Separate business and personal accounts

- [ ] Keep twelve months of cash-flow forecast
