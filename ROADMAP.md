# خارطة الطريق · Roadmap

The academy is built iteratively, in the open. Each iteration ships something a founder can use the same day. The order below is a plan, not a promise; contributions can move anything forward.

| Iteration | Theme | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| v0.1 | Foundation + Founder Toolkit | README, academy framework, course outlines, toolkit, lab, compliance tools | Released |
| **v0.2** | **Saudi Market Knowledge Framework** | Twelve-domain framework, regulatory register, official directory, founder compliance checklist, agentic governance playbook, founder-agent standard and first agent; open contribution guide | **This release** |
| v0.3 | Founder essentials in depth | Establishment walkthrough, PDPL worked example, tax basics, verified funding directory; Arabic versions of English-only guides | Next |
| v0.4 | Business development domains | IP, e-commerce, employment, procurement and sector guides | Planned |
| v0.5 | Founder agents and runnable templates | Remaining founder agents; workflow exports and reference code; Arabic evaluation sets | Planned |
| Later, in iterations | **Courses** | The four courses, built one at a time on top of the knowledge framework | Planned |
| v1.0 | Public launch | Website generated from the repository; project showcase | Planned |

## Why the knowledge framework comes before the courses

The courses teach what to do; the framework says how to do it in the Kingdom. Building the framework first means the courses can point to maintained, sourced material instead of repeating it. The four course outlines in `03-courses/` are drafts for discussion and will change.

## Known gaps in v0.2

- Seven of the twelve domains are at seed level: maps, decisions, checklists and a list of wanted guides.
- Many register entries are marked ◻ (to verify). Each is an open task.
- Regulatory summaries have not been reviewed by a Saudi lawyer; tax content has not been reviewed by a tax adviser.
- Deeper guides are in English with Arabic summaries.
- Case studies are illustrative scenarios until real ones are contributed.
- Agent templates are specifications, not runnable exports.

## Where help matters most right now

1. Verifying ◻ items in the [regulatory register](02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/regulatory-register.md).
2. Practitioner-written guides for tax, IP, employment and procurement.
3. Verified entries for the funding directory, ideally from programme operators.
4. Arabic versions of the published guides.
5. Real case studies.
