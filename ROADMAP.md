# خارطة الطريق · Roadmap

The academy is built iteratively, in the open. Each iteration ships something a founder can use the same day. The order below is a plan, not a promise; contributions can move anything forward.

## Content freeze (26 November 2026)

**No new content until a first cohort has run.** After eleven days of building, the Academy has four written courses, a twelve-domain framework, founder agents, runnable templates and a methodology library, and it has not yet been used by one founder. From v0.14 the work turns from writing to proving.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stops** | New lessons, decks, quizzes, library cards, methodology agents, case and venture pages, framework restorations, funding-directory entries, synthetic model runs |
| **Continues** | Corrections; what a first cohort needs (a one-page journey, three evidence gates, an impact ledger, one integration recipe); anything a real participant or business shows to be needed |
| **Measure from now on** | Problems validated, agents running in real businesses, paid pilots and paying customers of participants. Not courses, lessons, cards or commits |
| **First sector** | Order and customer-service operations for online stores |
| **Lifted when** | The first cohort's results are published |

Contributions of content are still welcome as issues; they are labelled `frozen-content` and taken up after the cohort reports. Corrections, reviews by specialists, a store willing to host a pilot, and builders for the cohort are wanted now.

## Why the knowledge framework comes before the courses

The courses teach what to do; the framework says how to do it in the Kingdom. Building the framework first means the courses can point to maintained, sourced material instead of repeating it. All four courses now have their first content; none has been piloted, and each will change with a first cohort.

## Known gaps in v0.14

- The runnable templates have met one small local model, outside n8n, on invented messages. No larger model, no run through n8n's HTTP node with a real model, no channel, no business. Since 25 November 2026 template 1 also looks for a second request in code, from the words listed in its Config; a request worded outside that list still depends on the model, and the model tried did not name one in either of two runs.

- Every domain now has at least one guide, but the five added in v0.7 each rest on one or two sources (a foreign government overview for IP, a press report for e-commerce, law-firm commentary for employment, an automated reading of the gazette text for procurement, and no new source for sector regulation). Each says so at its top. (The tax guide awaits an adviser's review and the establishment walkthrough a lawyer's.)
- The procurement guide found a law text on the gazette site that repeals the 2019 law; its decree number and effective date are not confirmed (C44).
- The regulatory register has no "to verify" rows since 20 November 2026. Several rows confirm only that an instrument exists and is in force, not what it requires. The same rule was applied to the twelve domain pages, the official directory and the two funding guides. A first batch was restored from official pages on 22 November 2026 (zakat and income tax; wage protection, work permits and the freelance document; the E-Commerce Law's articles; the two financial sandboxes; Biban and LEAP), and the rest is still out: see the [removal record](02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/not-adopted.md).
- Regulatory summaries have not been reviewed by a Saudi lawyer; tax content has not been reviewed by a tax adviser.
- Arabic versions of the deeper guides (domains 02, 03, 04, 06, 11) and the regulatory register are not lawyer-reviewed.
- The funding directory lists verified entries only since 20 November 2026 (31 verified, one marked inactive by its operator). Most entries state what the operator's page does not say: amounts, equity and eligibility are often missing. Items that could not be verified were removed and are listed in the framework's [removal record](02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/not-adopted.md).
- Case studies and venture journeys are summaries of public sources, none independently verified; no owner-written Saudi case yet. One venture source (the Aqar company page) could not be opened.
- Course 1 has slides, quizzes and practice interview records but has not been piloted. Course 2 has lessons, templates, a worked example, slides and quizzes, and has not been piloted. Course 3 has seven lessons, slide decks, quizzes, templates and a worked example, and has not been piloted; its Modules 3.4 and 3.7 have had no legal or tax review. Course 4 has six lessons, slide decks, quizzes, templates and a worked example, and has not been piloted; its Modules 4.3 and 4.4 have had no legal or security review.
- The lab exercises, the venture workbook and the instructor kit have not been tried with learners.
- The runnable agent templates and the low-code lab run with a keyword simulator. Their instructions were run once with a real model (Claude Fable 5.2, 21 November 2026, [results](05-agentic-ai-lab/agent-templates/workflows/real-model-runs/README.md)): 47 of 47 template cases after one fix, 32 of 35 and 8 of 8 on the lab. That run was made outside n8n, so the HTTP node with a real endpoint is still untested; no local or small model has been tried; no messaging channel, calendar, CRM or ledger is connected. Since 23 November 2026 their webhooks ask for a header token, checked inside n8n 2.35.7 but not behind a reverse proxy or a tunnel, and a shadow mode was added, run inside n8n on three of the five templates and not tried with a business.
- The twelve founder agents are definitions with specified test conversations. Agents 2 to 12 each have one [recorded run](02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/founder-agents/recorded-runs/README.md) with one assistant (Claude Fable 5.2, 21 November 2026): 30 of 33 conversations consistent with the expected behaviour, 3 not fully exercised for want of the input the case describes. None has been tried with a founder, and no second assistant has been used.
- There is no evaluation set from real Saudi-dialect messages: every test case in the repository is invented.

والدروس. **ما يأتي:** الإصدار 0.15 يجهّز لأول فوج: رحلة مشروع من أربع مراحل (اكتشف، ابنِ، بِع، شغّل) بثلاث بوابات تُجتاز بالدليل، وسجل للأثر، ووصفة ربط واحدة للمتاجر الإلكترونية من وثائق مزوّديها،
