## Why the knowledge framework comes before the courses

The courses teach what to do; the framework says how to do it in the Kingdom. Building the framework first means the courses can point to maintained, sourced material instead of repeating it. Course 1 now has its first content; the outlines for Courses 2 to 4 in `03-courses/` are drafts for discussion and will change.

## Known gaps in v0.5

- Six of the twelve domains are at seed level: maps, decisions, checklists and a list of wanted guides. (Tax gained its first guide on 18 November 2026, awaiting an adviser's review.)
- Many register entries are marked ◻ (to verify). Each is an open task.
- Regulatory summaries have not been reviewed by a Saudi lawyer; tax content has not been reviewed by a tax adviser.
- Arabic versions of the deeper guides (domains 02, 03, 04, 06, 11) and the regulatory register are not lawyer-reviewed.
- The funding directory still has entries marked "to verify".
- Case studies are summaries of public sources, none independently verified; no owner-written Saudi case yet.
- Course 1 has no slides or quizzes and has not been piloted; Courses 2 to 4 are outlines.
- The lab exercises have not been tried with learners.
- Agent templates are specifications, not runnable exports.

## Where help matters most right now

1. Verifying ◻ items in the [regulatory register](02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/regulatory-register.md).
2. Practitioner-written guides for tax, IP, employment and procurement.
3. Verified entries for the funding directory, ideally from programme operators.
4. Review of the Arabic versions by a lawyer and by native-speaking practitioners.
5. Real case studies.
