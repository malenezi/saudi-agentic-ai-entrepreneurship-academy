## مستويات الدليل

كل حالة تشغيل تحمل وسماً واحداً. **لا توجد حتى الآن حالة تحقق منها طرف مستقل**، وكل الأرقام المنشورة هنا معلنة من الشركة أو من مزوّد حلّها.

| الوسم | المعنى | كيف تستخدمها |
|---|---|---|
| **أ · أرقام من الشركة نفسها** | الشركة صاحبة التجربة نشرت الأرقام والتفاصيل | مرجع تعليمي؛ اذكر أن الأرقام معلنة ذاتياً |
| **ب · أرقام من المزوّد** | الحالة حقيقية، والناشر هو من باع الحل أو البنية | اقرأها كما تقرأ مادة تسويقية: ما الذي لم يُذكر؟ |
| **ج · إعلان بلا نتائج** | نشر أو شراكة معلنة دون أرقام قابلة للقياس | مادة لتمرين استخراج سير العمل، لا دليل على عائد |
| **سيناريو توضيحي** | منشأة متخيَّلة وأرقام افتراضية | تدريب فقط؛ لا تستشهد به كدليل سوقي |

## In English

Three kinds of page. **Operating cases** (fifteen: eight Saudi, seven international) summarise, from public sources, an organisation that put an agent to work; each carries an evidence level: **A** figures published by the company itself, **B** figures published by its vendor, **C** announcement without measurable results. **No case here has been independently verified.** **[Venture pages](ventures/README.md)** (twenty-two) are sourced accounts of companies: ten Saudi companies building for agents, five international ones, and seven on how technology companies in this market were built, funded and exited; they show where money is being bet and are not evidence about agents. **[Illustrative scenarios](illustrative-scenarios.md)** (six) are invented and must not be cited as market evidence.

The index above is in three parts: the cases by place with what the agent does, the venture pages by group, and a table that picks cases by the topic and course module being taught. Claims from drafts that could not be confirmed are listed in [not-adopted.md](not-adopted.md). Every figure is registered in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).

Added on 22 November 2026: Panda, Nana, Saudia Cargo, King Faisal Specialist Hospital (published as a comparison case, because its own releases describe forecasting and alerting, not an agent), HUMAIN and Replit, Orange and Nexus; and ten venture pages (Shaffra, RIME, Gaia, TabSense, Velents.ai, Sierra, Wonderful, Harvey, Rocketlane, 11x).

There are two kinds of case study in this folder:

1. **Public-source summaries** (the fifteen above): short, paraphrased, linked to the original. They follow the short format used in the files.
2. **Contributed cases**: written with the business owner. They follow the full template below.

## Template for contributed cases

Maximum two pages.

```markdown
# (Title: sector, job, outcome)

**Status:** Real case · Published with the owner's permission on (date)
**Sector / city / size:** ...
**Named or anonymised:** ...

## 1. The business problem
What was happening, how often, at what cost. In the owner's words where possible.

## 2. The workflow before
Steps, people, systems, time. Where errors and delays occurred.

## 3. The agent
What it does end to end. Architecture (workflow, one agent, several). Tools and
channel. Model type (hosted or self-hosted) and where data is processed.

## 4. Human oversight
What needs approval, what is forbidden, how that is enforced.

## 5. Implementation
Who built it, how long it took, what was harder than expected.

## 6. Economics
Cost per resolved task, success rate, price or internal saving.
State how each number was measured and over what period.

## 7. Results
Before and after, with dates. What did not improve.

## 8. Compliance and data
Personal data involved, how it is handled, issues met.

## 9. Lessons
Three things to repeat, three things to do differently.

## 10. Evidence
What the reviewer saw: logs, reports, a demo, a customer confirmation.
```

## Rules for contributors

- **Permission first** for contributed cases. Written agreement from the business owner.
- **Public-source summaries** need a link to the original publisher, an evidence tag, and a "what the source does not say" section. Paraphrase; do not copy.
- **No personal data** and no confidential figures the owner has not cleared.
- **Measured, not estimated.** Say how every number was obtained and who published it. If it is an estimate, label it.
- **Include what failed.** A case study with no failures is an advertisement.
- **No promotion** of the builder's company beyond a one-line credit.
