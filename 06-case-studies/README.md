## Current status

There are no real case studies yet. What is published are [three illustrative scenarios](illustrative-scenarios.md): **invented** businesses with **assumed** numbers, written to show the format and the reasoning. Do not cite them as market evidence. They will be replaced as real cases are contributed.

## Case study template

Every real case study follows this structure. Maximum two pages.

```markdown
# (Title: sector, job, outcome)

**Status:** Real case · Published with the owner's permission on (date)
**Sector / city / size:** ...
**Named or anonymised:** ...

## 1. The business problem
What was happening, how often, at what cost. In the owner's words where possible.

## 2. The workflow before
Steps, people, systems, time. Where errors and delays occurred.

## 3. The agent
What it does end to end. Architecture (workflow, one agent, several). Tools and
channel. Model type (hosted or self-hosted) and where data is processed.

## 4. Human oversight
What needs approval, what is forbidden, how that is enforced.

## 5. Implementation
Who built it, how long it took, what was harder than expected.

## 6. Economics
Cost per resolved task, success rate, price or internal saving.
State how each number was measured and over what period.

## 7. Results
Before and after, with dates. What did not improve.

## 8. Compliance and data
Personal data involved, how it is handled, issues met.

## 9. Lessons
Three things to repeat, three things to do differently.

## 10. Evidence
What the reviewer saw: logs, reports, a demo, a customer confirmation.
```

## Rules for contributors

- **Permission first.** Written agreement from the business owner.
- **No personal data** and no confidential figures the owner has not cleared.
- **Measured, not estimated.** Say how every number was obtained. If it is an estimate, label it.
- **Include what failed.** A case study with no failures is an advertisement.
- **No promotion** of the builder's company beyond a one-line credit.
