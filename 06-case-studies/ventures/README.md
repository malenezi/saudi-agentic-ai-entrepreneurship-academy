## من أين جاءت

## كيف تُستخدم

1. اقرأ «ما هو موثّق» ثم «ما لا تقوله المصادر». الفرق بينهما هو الدرس الأول.
2. أجب عن أسئلة الحالة كتابةً قبل النقاش.
3. «زاوية لمؤسس وكيلي» أسئلة للبحث، وليست ادعاءات عن الشركة.

## In English

Seven short, sourced accounts of how technology companies in this market were built, funded and exited. They serve Courses 3 and 4 and are **not** evidence about agents: none claims the company uses agents in its operations unless a source says so. The company list comes from the author's earlier university course on software entrepreneurship (2024); those texts carried no sources and some contained factual errors, so every case was rewritten from open sources and what could not be confirmed is recorded in [not adopted](../not-adopted.md). Each case lists what is documented, what the sources do not say, the lesson, questions for an agentic founder, and learner questions.
