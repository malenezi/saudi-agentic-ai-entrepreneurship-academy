**Date:** 20 November 2026. **Scope:** every [source register](../../SOURCES.md) entry cited by Course 1 and Course 2 materials. **Method:** each cited page was opened again and the recorded fact compared with what the page says. An automated pass also compared every figure quoted in the lessons, briefs and decks with its register entry.

- A26, A36, A44: the first source of each was re-opened; the second page of each (and the standards reference in A44) was not.
- A43: Salla's page was re-opened; the pages for the other three products were not.
