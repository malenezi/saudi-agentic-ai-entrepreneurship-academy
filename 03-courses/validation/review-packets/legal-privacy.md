## 3. Questions

1. Is the controller and processor analysis in lesson 3.4 correct for a founder who runs an agent on behalf of a merchant, and for one who offers a service under their own name?
2. Lesson 3.4 presents breach notification to the authority as conditional on likely harm, within 72 hours, from an unofficial copy of the regulation (A50), and labels the unconditional version as a draft (A52). Is that the position in force today?
3. Are the lawful bases described accurately, in particular the limits on legitimate interest (A50)?
4. Is the description of transfer outside the Kingdom in lessons 2.4 and 3.4 accurate and current? Which instrument governs it today?
5. Do the registration rules (A111) and the data protection officer rules (A110) apply as the lesson says to a small processor serving merchants? Both entries rest on summaries.
6. Penalties (A109) are stated from law-firm summaries. Are the amounts and conditions right?
7. Is the consent script in lesson 1.4 adequate for observing a workflow and interviewing staff?
8. Does the pilot and LOI outline create obligations a founder would not expect? What must be added before it is used as a template?
9. Is anything in these lessons likely to be read as legal advice despite the disclaimers? Where should a "see a lawyer" line be added?
10. What is missing that a first-time founder selling to Saudi businesses must be told?
