## 3. Questions

1. Are the VAT registration thresholds and the difference between mandatory and voluntary registration stated correctly (A53)?
2. Is the latest e-invoicing wave, its revenue threshold and its date stated correctly (A54)? What has changed since 18 November 2026?
3. Lesson 3.7 says registration with ZATCA follows commercial registration automatically, and that this is different from VAT registration (A65, A53). Is that accurate?
4. Is the statement on capital for a limited liability company correct (A69, A71)?
5. Are the commercial register confirmation and update periods under the Commercial Register Law stated correctly (A114, read through summaries)?
6. Lesson 3.7 leaves "may VAT be charged before the registration certificate" as a question for the adviser. What is the answer, and should the lesson state it?
7. Is the comparison of legal forms a fair orientation for a solo founder and for two co-founders? What is misleading by omission?
8. Which fees or periods quoted in the establishment walkthrough are out of date?
9. What is missing on zakat, withholding tax or social insurance that a founder hiring a first employee must know?
