## Before you submit

- [ ] A non-technical owner can read it without a glossary
- [ ] Every number has an assumption or a source next to it
- [ ] Evidence is behaviour and quotes, not opinions
- [ ] No market size copied from a report; reachable market is bottom-up
- [ ] The approval gate is named
- [ ] The five [validation gates](../lessons/1-6-scoring-and-economics.md) are all "yes"
- [ ] No names or personal data
