# نواتج التعلّم والتقييم · Outcomes and assessment alignment

For each course: every learning outcome, the modules that teach it, the quizzes that check it formatively, and the rubric criteria that assess it in the deliverable. A test checks that every outcome is taught by a module and assessed by a criterion, and that every criterion assesses an outcome. The outcome-to-criterion mapping is a design judgement; the module mapping is read from the lesson headers.

## Prerequisite chain

| Course | Requires | Which is the deliverable of |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | A documented opportunity: named customer role, mapped workflow, five interviews | Course 1 |
| 3 | A working, evaluated agent with a tested stop switch | Course 2 |

Each course ends where the next begins. The entry checks are in the [trainer guide](TRAINER-GUIDE.md), section 2, and the completion rules in the [assessment standard](assessment-standard.md).
