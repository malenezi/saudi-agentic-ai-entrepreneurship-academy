# دليل المدرب · Instructor guide

For anyone running Course 3 as a cohort. Self-paced learners can skip this file. Nothing here has been piloted yet; tell us what happened when you run it.

## Before the first session

- Every learner arrives with a working, evaluated agent from Course 2 or an equivalent: a test set, a measured success rate, an oversight matrix and a tested stop switch. A learner with a demo and no measurements goes back to Course 2, Modules 2.5 and 2.6.
- **Each learner needs one real business willing to use the agent for two weeks.** This is the scarce resource of the course. Ask for it four weeks before the start, and help learners who have none through the interviewees from Course 1.
- **A written agreement with that business before any real personal data is processed**: what data, where it is processed, who the providers are, deletion at the end. One page is enough. Validation gate 3 depends on it.
- The instructor is not a lawyer or a tax adviser, and should say so in the first session. Modules 3.4 and 3.7 teach what to ask and what to document. If a lawyer or an accountant can give an hour to the cohort, put it after Module 3.4 and after Module 3.7.
- Open the official pages used in Modules 3.4 and 3.7 in the week before delivery. Rules, fees and dates change. The checks are listed at the end of this file.

## Delivery schedules (20 contact hours)

The live deployment needs calendar time: two weeks of use, then two to three weeks for pilot offers and replies. Plan the cohort over six to eight weeks.

| Format | Session | Modules | Between sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Five half-days** | S1 (4h) | 3.1 (3h); 3.2 concepts (1h) | **Deploy.** Week one of live use, staff sends every reply |
| | S2 (4h) | 3.2 canvas and pricing model (2h); 3.4 roles, seven questions, data map (2h) | Week two of live use. Self-assessment and provider assessments |
| | S3 (4h) | 3.3 with the two weeks of real numbers (3h); 3.4 buyers, security, open issues (1h) | Pricing memo. Buyer evidence pack. Twenty names |
| | S4 (4h) | 3.4 files reviewed in pairs (1h); 3.5 (3h) | Contact five to ten businesses. Record the funnel |
| | S5 (4h) | 3.6 (2h); 3.7 (2h) | Send three pilot offers. Submit the pack two to three weeks later |
| **Ten evenings** | 10 × 2h | Same order; 3.1, 3.3, 3.4 and 3.5 take two evenings each, with 3.4 split around 3.3 | Same work between sessions |

Module 3.4 starts before Module 3.3 on purpose: the data map must exist before more real data flows, and Module 3.3 needs the second week's numbers. Allow at least a week between S1 and S2, and between S2 and S3.

## Notes by module

- **3.1.** The commonest failure is a pilot user who is the founder's friend or relative. Ask in the first hour who the user is and how the learner knows them. The second is deploying without having pressed the stop switch; have each learner do it in the room.
- **3.2.** Learners choose a pricing model in minutes and skip the definition of the unit. Spend the time there: have each pair try to dispute the other's definition of "resolved". The service-or-software decision is the real content of the module; a learner who cannot say who handles an escalation has not made it.
- **3.3.** Run this module only on the learner's own log. With no live numbers, use the held-back success rate from Course 2 and mark every figure as an estimate. Expect surprise that the model is a small share of cost; let the learner find it in their own numbers before showing the example. Check that no one improves the margin by deleting review.
- **3.4.** Keep the group out of legal argument. The exercise is documentation: role, map, providers, locations, open issues. A learner who writes "not settled, owner, date" has done the work; a learner who writes "compliant" has not. Read aloud the difference between the rule in force and the October 2026 draft, and check that no file presents the draft as current.
- **3.5.** Do not accept a plan without twenty named businesses. Outreach is sent by the learner, in person, during the week. Role-play the first message with one learner as the owner of a busy shop who has thirty seconds.
- **3.6.** Have pairs swap offers and try to judge the pilot from the page alone. If the reader has to ask what the number is or where it is counted, the measure is not ready. Refusals are deliverables: a learner with three recorded refusals and their reasons has met the requirement.
- **3.7.** Orientation only, and the shortest module for a reason. Open the service pages live. Where a page differs from the lesson, the page is right; ask the learner who found it to open an issue. Do not answer "which legal form should I choose".

## What a facilitator should not do

- Give legal or tax conclusions, or approve a learner's compliance position.
- Supply a pilot business from their own network without a written agreement of the kind above.
- Accept invented customers, references or results "to complete the exercise". Gate 4 has no exceptions.
- Let a learner process real customer data in a tool that has not been through the provider assessment.
