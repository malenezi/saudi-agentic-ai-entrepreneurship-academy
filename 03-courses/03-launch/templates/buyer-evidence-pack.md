# ملف أدلة المشتري · Buyer evidence pack

What a medium or large customer asks for before it signs, gathered in one file you can send the day it is requested. Used in [Module 3.4](../lessons/3-4-compliance-readiness.md). It collects documents built elsewhere; it does not replace the [PDPL self-assessment](../../../02-saudi-market-knowledge-framework/compliance-toolkit/pdpl-self-assessment.md) or a lawyer's review.
