# مذكرة التسعير · Pricing memo

One page that records the price, what it rests on and what would change it. Used in [Module 3.3](../lessons/3-3-unit-economics.md). The numbers come from your own log and the [calculator](../../../04-entrepreneur-toolkit/cost_per_resolved_task.py); a worked version is in the [example](../examples/worked-example-launch-pack.md).
