## ثلاثة أرقام كل أسبوع

1. **تكلفة المهمة المنجزة:** من حاسبة `cost_per_resolved_task.py` في [حقيبة المؤسس](../04-entrepreneur-toolkit/README.md).
2. **نسبة ما أنجزه الوكيل دون تصعيد،** ونسبة ما صُعِّد وكان التصعيد في محله.
3. **ما رآه العميل:** شكوى، أو تصحيح، أو شكر. بنصّه.

رقم لا يتغير أسبوعين متتاليين يعني أن دورة التحسين توقفت.

## English summary

Day 90 to day 180, ending at the day-180 follow-up. You leave with a retained customer or a measured saving, and a decision made on numbers: scale, hold or stop. Not yet tested.

- **Every two weeks, operating clinic:** review the KPI sheet and failure log with a peer. The trend counts, not a snapshot.
- **Day 120, second customer:** repeat the sale without the Academy. A second signed pilot, or a documented loss with its reasons.
- **Day 150, security review:** an agent security assessment with a practitioner; findings closed.
- **Day 180, decision:** a one-page memo with revenue, cost and retention, confirmed by the customer; and a case study written by you.

**Three numbers each week:** cost per resolved task; the share the agent completed without escalation, and the share escalated rightly; and what the customer saw, verbatim. A number unchanged for two weeks means the improvement loop has stopped.
