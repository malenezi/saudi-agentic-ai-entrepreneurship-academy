## كيف يسير الأسبوع

جلسة عمل واحدة من ثلاث ساعات، ثم ست إلى ثماني ساعات في الميدان أو أمام الحاسب. الدروس مادة مساندة تُقرأ حين تحتاج إليها البوابة، لا قبل ذلك. وفي كل أسبوع مُخرج واحد يُقيَّم بما يراه المقيِّم: ملاحظات مؤرخة، أو تقرير تولّده الأدوات، أو توقيع، أو فاتورة.

يبدأ أول فوج بقطاع واحد: **عمليات الطلبات وخدمة العملاء في المتاجر الإلكترونية.** فيه أنضج ما في المستودع: الوكيل المرجعي للطلبات، والقالب 1 لخدمة العملاء، و[وضع الظل](../05-agentic-ai-lab/agent-templates/workflows/shadow-mode/README.md)، و[المتجر المختلق](../05-agentic-ai-lab/datasets/ecommerce-store/README.md)، و[ورقة شروط التجربة المدفوعة](../04-entrepreneur-toolkit/14-paid-pilot-terms-sheet.md). القطاعات المنظَّمة (الصحة، والمالية، والمشتريات الحكومية) خارج الفوج الأول.

## علاقة الرحلة بالمقررات الأربعة

المقررات باقية في مكانها، ومن أراد دراستها بالترتيب فله ذلك ([فهرس المقررات](../03-courses/README.md)). الرحلة تعيد ترتيبها حول المشروع: المقرر 1 للمرحلة الأولى، والمقرر 2 للثانية، والمقرر 3 للثالثة مع تقديم التسعير والتجربة المدفوعة إلى ما قبل البناء، ومن المقرر 4 دروس التشغيل والمراقبة والأمن للمرحلة الرابعة. أما التمويل والعرض الاستثماري فلمن صار له عميل يدفع.

## English

**The Academy's main path.** Twelve weeks from a real problem inside a Saudi business to a paying customer. Progress is decided at three gates passed on evidence, not by lessons completed.

> **Not yet tested.** Designed on 26 November 2026 from the material of the four existing courses. No participant has taken it. A first cohort is planned after Eid al-Fitr 2027, and the journey will change with what it shows.

| Stage | When | You leave with | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. Find](1-find.md) | Weeks 1 to 4 | A problem a named business will pay to solve | **Gate 1:** problem evidence |
| [2. Build](2-build.md) | Weeks 5 to 8 | An agent doing the real work beside staff | **Gate 2:** agent in shadow mode |
| [3. Sell](3-sell.md) | Weeks 9 to 12 | A paid pilot on signed terms | **Gate 3:** a business has paid |
| [4. Operate](4-operate.md) | Day 90 to day 180 | A retained customer or a measured saving | Day-180 follow-up |

Criteria and evidence for each gate are in [the three gates](gates.md); results are recorded in the [impact ledger](../impact/README.md).

**A week** is one three-hour working session and six to eight hours in the field or at the keyboard. Lessons are support material, read when a gate calls for them.

**Three routes.** Founder on the low-code track (n8n templates); founder on the coding track (the Python reference agent); adopter, an owner or employee deploying an agent inside an existing business, who takes Find, Build and Operate and succeeds with a saving the owner confirms.

**Graduation is a statement about evidence.** *Validated:* Gate 1 passed, or a documented no-go with the evidence that killed the idea. *Builder:* Gate 2. *Founder:* Gate 3. *Adopter:* Gates 1 and 2, then thirty days live with a measured saving. There is no certificate of attendance.

**First sector.** Order and customer-service operations for online stores. Regulated sectors (health, finance, government procurement) are outside the first cohort.

**Agents from week one.** Five of the founder agents draft every artifact: a scout, a rehearsal partner, a regulatory navigator, an architect and an economist. The founder verifies and signs. No agent acts for a customer before five working days in shadow mode, and every irreversible action passes an approval enforced by the workflow, not by the model.

**The four courses stay where they are.** The journey reorders them around the venture: Course 1 for Find, Course 2 for Build, Course 3 for Sell with pricing and the paid pilot brought forward, and the operating, monitoring and security lessons of Course 4 for Operate. Funding and the investor pitch are for ventures that already have a paying customer.
