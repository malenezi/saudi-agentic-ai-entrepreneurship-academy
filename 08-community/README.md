## English

**Ways to take part:** use the material and open a `feedback` issue; add your project to the showcase by pull request; write a one-page founder story; contribute a case study with the [template](../06-case-studies/README.md); run a cohort at your university, incubator or club and report back; review, proofread and translate.

**Showcase entry requirement:** a working agent, an evaluation set and customer evidence (a pilot, an LOI or usage). Ideas alone are not listed.

**Running a cohort.** Any organisation may deliver these courses under the licence terms. We ask three things: credit the academy, keep the "no number without a source" rule, and share anonymised outcomes so the material improves.

**Maintainer.** Founded and maintained by Prof. Mamdouh Alenezi in a personal capacity. The initiative is independent and does not represent any organisation.
